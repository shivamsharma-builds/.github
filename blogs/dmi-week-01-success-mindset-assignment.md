# DMI - Week 01 - Success Mindset Assignment

## Introduction

Week 01 focuses on habits that support consistent technical growth.

## Key lessons

- Learn by building, not only by watching.
- Break complex problems into smaller experiments.
- Document what you learn.
- Treat errors as feedback.
- Practice consistently.

DevOps is a continuous-learning field. A strong learning process means experimenting, observing results, understanding failures, and improving.
